A living field record

One forest. Four censuses. A rare view of ecological change.

San Emilio lies within Area de Conservacion Guanacaste on Costa Rica's northwestern Pacific slope. Strong wet-dry seasonality, varied terrain, and a mosaic of forest ages make the plot a natural observatory for understanding tropical dry forest dynamics.

Repeated mapping of woody stems links individual trees through time. The record supports research on population turnover, functional composition, drought response, canopy phenology, and the legacies of land use and disturbance.
