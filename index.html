Plot overview

Site setting and sampling record

The San Emilio Forest Dynamics Plot is in Sector Santa Rosa of Área de Conservación Guanacaste, Guanacaste Province, Costa Rica, at 10.839379° N, 85.613550° W. It is seasonally dry tropical forest with a wet season from June through December, a dry season from January through May, and approximately 1,500 mm of annual rainfall.

The plot includes ridge, slope, mesa-top, and valley-bottom habitats. The ForestGEO profile describes roughly one-third as approximately 100-year-old secondary forest on land formerly used for cattle grazing and banana cultivation, another third as likely 120–170 years old, and the remainder as intermediate in age. These are inferred relative age classes rather than exact establishment dates.

ForestGEO profile metadata report approximately 34,000 trees and 176 species. The profile does not define whether these totals include lianas, multiple stems, unidentified taxa, or only accepted tree species. The project presentation separately reports 36,465 stems at least 1 cm DBH and 145 species for the recent survey; the totals should not be treated as interchangeable until their counting and taxonomic definitions are reconciled.

ForestGEO lists a census area of 15.64 hectares and nominal dimensions of 240 × 680 m. A complete rectangle with those dimensions would cover 16.32 hectares, so the dimensions are presented here as approximate bounds rather than an area calculation. Earlier surveys covered a reported 14.4 hectares.

Source: ForestGEO San Emilio profile, accessed 31 July 2026.

Survey history

Census methods and changing inclusion thresholds

  1. 1976–77

    Initial mapped survey

    George Stevens and Stephen Hubbell censused 14.4 hectares, mapping and measuring all woody stems, including lianas, at least 3 cm DBH at 1.3 m. ForestGEO identifies this as the 1976 census.

  2. 1995–96

    First recensus

    Brian Enquist led a recensus using the same 3 cm DBH threshold and a broadly comparable mapped-survey design. The original computer-card data were digitized in 1995 and quality checked by Stevens and Enquist.

  3. 2006–07

    Second recensus

    Nathan Swenson led the third survey using the 3 cm DBH threshold. Mapping and tagging procedures were not identical across all survey years.

  4. 2019–21

    ForestGEO census

    Adam Chmurzynski led a ForestGEO-protocol census of 15.64 hectares, mapping all woody stems, including lianas, at least 1 cm DBH.

The site has four mapped woody-stem surveys, but only the 2019–21 survey used the ForestGEO protocol. Earlier surveys used an overlapping historical design, a 3 cm threshold, and a smaller reported area.

Resources

Data sources and contact

Compilation and collection of San Emilio Long Term Forest Dynamics Plot (SEFDP) data are coordinated and provided by PI Brian J. Enquist and Co-PIs Nathan Swenson and Adam Chmurzynski.

The ForestGEO plot profile is publicly available, and the full SEFDP dataset is available by request. We welcome inquiries and ask interested researchers to contact the SEFDP PI team with a short summary of their project and research interests. The team will follow up to coordinate data access. Before the full dataset is shared, data users complete a data use agreement.

Data-use summary

  • Use is limited to the described project and purposes permitted by the signed agreement.
  • Data may not be redistributed beyond the project collaboration team identified in the agreement.
  • Metadata, source information, and license terms must accompany permitted copies.
  • Derived works remain subject to the applicable agreement terms.
  • Publications must acknowledge SEFDP and required institutional and funding support. Collaboration and authorship should be discussed with the SEFDP PI team; authorship is not automatic and depends on active, timely scholarly contributions.

This summary is provided for convenience and does not replace or modify the signed data use agreement.
